# Verified lead sheet — Marietta / south Cobb

Compiled 6 November 2026, after the Yogardener correction. Every business
below was checked against its **actual web presence**, not a listing field.

## Method

1. **Candidates.** Google Maps, ten categories around Marietta (landscaping,
   lawn care, barbershop, hair braiding, pressure washing, auto repair,
   handyman, house cleaning, tree service, pet grooming). 347 raw results,
   filtered to rating ≥ 4.0, ≥ 8 reviews, a south-Cobb address, chains and
   franchises dropped → 58 independents.
2. **Website check.** Each business's Google **place page** was opened and read
   for its website field, including the "Add website" button — which appears
   only when a place has no website at all. Where Maps was inconclusive, the
   business was independently web-searched and any candidate domain fetched
   and confirmed by name and phone.

## The headline

**Among established independents, "no website" barely exists.** Of 24
businesses checked, 19 have real websites of their own. The five that do not
are all barbershops or braiding salons, and every one of them still runs on
Instagram plus a booking platform (Booksy, GlossGenius, Vagaro). One
barbershop runs on a free ChatGPT site.

**The addressable segment is not "no website" — it is "no website of their
own": businesses living on Instagram and a booking-platform page.** That is a
real, nameable pitch: a page that is theirs, that ranks, that they own.

## What was wrong with the old scan

The old check trusted the Maps *listing*, and got it wrong in both directions:
Yogardener was flagged "no website" while running a full WordPress site, and
Georgia Landscaping & Hardscaping was flagged "no website" while running
georgialandscapingandhardscapingllc.com. Two of the four sample leads were
wrong — a 50% error rate.

The reliable signals, in order of strength:

1. **The "Add website" button on the place page** — Google's own statement
   that the place has no site. This is the cheapest true-positive signal and
   should be the pipeline's primary test.
2. **An independent web search for the exact business name + city**, fetching
   any candidate domain and confirming name and phone. This catches the
   Yogardener case — a site the listing doesn't show.
3. A listing with no website field alone is **not** evidence of anything.

## Where the pool actually is

If "no website of their own" stays the criterion, the productive search is
**not** established businesses with 200+ reviews — they have sites. It is:

- barbershops and braiding salons (the whole category runs on Instagram +
  Booksy/GlossGenius/Vagaro)
- businesses under ~50 reviews, i.e. newer or smaller
- anyone on a free `chatgpt.site`, `business.site`, or booking-platform page

That is a smaller, more specific list than "10 categories × 15 businesses,"
and the pitch to it is different: not "you need a website" but "your
Instagram page is not a website — here is one that is yours, and it answers
your phone when you are holding scissors."
